1) Gold fingers are on ONE edge only: the edge with the 78.4 mm wide tab (30 fingers on each side). Please bevel this edge 30 degrees.
2) Edge rails for assembly: please add the two 5 mm rails on the LEFT and RIGHT edges (the two 85.7 mm edges, perpendicular to the gold-finger edge). The panel size must be 99.6 x 85.7 mm (not 89.6 x 95.7 mm). Do NOT put a rail on the gold-finger edge or on the opposite edge.
3) The gold-finger edge must stay a free routed edge: no rail, no V-cut, no mouse bites.
4) If you cannot add the rails on these two edges, or cannot bevel a board with rails: do NOT move the rails; put the order on hold and contact me.
5) You may shorten the finger copper to your standard setback for the bevel.
6) The solder mask window over the gold fingers is intentional: it starts 4.0 mm below the root of the tab and runs to the edge. Please do not enlarge it towards the board: tracks run beside the upper ends of the fingers.
7) The outline has two side notches, one inner slot (6.6 x 2.4 mm) and cut corners beside the tab. Inner corners are drawn with R 0.5 mm. Please keep them as drawn.
