1) Raspberry Pi Pico 2 module, JLCPCB part C41407547 (your part page says "SMT Assembly", "Standard Only", "X-ray Inspection: Required"). Each board carries three of them, soldered flat on the top side by their castellated edge pads.

There is no pad at the module's TP7, and we keep copper away from it. On U27 we use TP2, TP3 (USB) and TP6 (BOOTSEL) as electrical connections. Will these six pads get stencil apertures and be soldered in the same reflow, and will no paste be printed at TP7?
1d) Raspberry Pi's datasheet (section 3.2, Figure 6) requires stencil apertures of 3.8 x 2.2 mm on the 40 edge pads, larger than the 3.2 x 1.6 mm pads. Do you apply this for C41407547 by default? 3) Edge rails, tooling holes and fiducials: we have drawn two 5 mm rails ourselves, on the left and right edges of the 220 x 150 mm body (the two edges perpendicular to the gold-finger edge), so that the bevelled gold-finger edge stays an outer edge. The rails run the full height, from the tip of the gold fingers to the top edge, and are joined to the body by mouse bites: a 2 mm routed slot with five tabs per side (5.5 mm at the narrowest), each tab with seven 0.6 mm non-plated holes, 0.3 mm apart, just inside the body's edge (the end holes keep 0.37 mm of board from the slots' round ends). Each rail carries two 2.0 mm non-plated tooling holes (centred on the rail, 5 mm from its ends), and there are 3 fiducials (1 mm copper, 2 mm mask opening) on the rails, centred 3.85 mm from the outer edge and level with the first and last tabs. The size is then 234 x 162 mm.
3a) Is this acceptable for Standard PCBA with "Edge Rails/Fiducials: Added by Customer" (and "Tooling holes: Added by Customer")? In particular:

- The tooling holes sit on those free ends. Below the first tab the rail's inner edge is routed too, so each hole has 1.5 mm of rail on both sides, next to a milling path. Is that strong enough?
- Each fiducial's copper is 3.35 mm from the outer edge, but 2.8 mm from the round end of the slot beside the tab (its mask opening 2.3 mm). Is that acceptable?
3b) Should the order use Delivery Format "Single PCB" or "Panel by Customer" (1 x 1), and what should we enter as "Different Design"? Will the two rails be counted as additional designs, for the PCB or for the PCBA setup fee ("$25.56 x (number of designs -1)" on your price page)?

3d) Our gold fingers start 1.6 mm from the board edge. Your gold-finger page gives a copper setback of about 1.13 mm for a 30 degree bevel on 1.6 mm (about 0.74 mm at 45 degrees), and the order page shows a residual thickness of 0.53 mm.
